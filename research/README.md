# Project Summary

## Title
Reducing Cognitive Load in Institutional Systems: A Human-Centered Approach to Process Navigation

## Research Question
How can complex university processes be translated into clear, step-by-step workflows that reduce confusion and improve usability?

## Motivation
University systems are often difficult to navigate, especially for students who are unfamiliar with institutional language or processes. This creates confusion, delays, and stress when trying to complete essential tasks like registration or financial aid.

As a first-generation and technical student, I have observed how unclear systems create unnecessary barriers for users who are simply trying to complete required tasks.

## Approach
This project identifies common student pain points and translates complex workflows into structured, step-by-step processes.

Instead of presenting large amounts of information at once, the system focuses on:
- breaking tasks into clear actions  
- guiding users through decision points  
- reducing ambiguity  

## System Design
The solution is a web-based application that:
- presents processes in a structured format  
- highlights key steps and requirements  
- simplifies navigation through administrative systems  

The design prioritizes clarity, accessibility, and usability over completeness.

## Evaluation
To evaluate the effectiveness of the system, users were asked to compare traditional university process descriptions with the structured workflow approach.

Feedback focused on:
- clarity  
- ease of understanding  
- confidence in completing tasks  

Users consistently found the step-by-step format easier to follow and less overwhelming.

## Key Insights
- Users prefer guided workflows over dense instructions  
- Reducing cognitive load improves confidence and usability  
- Clear structure is more important than providing all information at once  

## Limitations
- Small sample size  
- Focused on one institutional context  
- Prototype does not integrate with live systems  

## Future Work
- Add AI-assisted guidance to dynamically support users  
- Personalize workflows based on user needs  
- Expand to additional university processes
